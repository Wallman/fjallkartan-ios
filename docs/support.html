Fjällkartan · Topographic maps for Norway and Sweden

Questions, bug reports and feature requests are welcome. Email fjallkartan@wallman.dev and you will get a reply. Including your device model and iOS version helps.

Frequently asked questions

Where does the map data come from?

Norway is served by Kartverket and Sweden by Lantmäteriet, the national mapping agencies. The two are composited so the border area is continuous.

How do I use the map offline?

Open the offline regions sheet, pan and zoom until the area you want fills the screen, then download it. The download covers zoom levels 7–14, which is enough to read terrain and follow a route. Very large areas are refused, so download a few smaller regions instead of one huge one.

My download stopped part way through.

Resuming picks up where it left off — tiles already stored are skipped. If it reports a failure, check that the device has free storage.

How do I delete downloaded maps?

Delete the region from the offline regions sheet. Tiles shared with another region you still have are kept; everything else is removed.

How do I measure a distance?

Turn on the ruler and trace your route with a finger. The map stops panning while the ruler is on. Lift your finger to pan or zoom, then draw again — the segments are joined automatically. Distances are measured along the ground, not on the flat map, so they are correct even in the far north.

Is my location shared?

No. It is used only to draw your position on the map on your device. See the privacy policy.

The map is blank in some areas.

Outside Norway and Sweden there is no coverage. Inside them, a blank tile usually means no network connection and no downloaded region for that area.
