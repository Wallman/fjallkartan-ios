Privacy Policy

Fjällkartan · Last updated 6 August 2026

Fjällkartan does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. The app contains no third-party SDKs.

Location

If you grant location access, your position is used solely to draw your location on the map on your device. It is never transmitted, never stored and never shared with us or anyone else. You can decline location access and the app remains fully usable.

Data stored on your device

The following stays on your device and is never sent anywhere:

  • Map areas you download for offline use
  • Routes you measure
  • App settings

Deleting the app removes all of it.

Map data from third parties

Map imagery is supplied by the national mapping agencies of Norway and Sweden. When the app is online and needs a map tile it has not already downloaded, it requests that tile directly from:

  • Kartverket (Norway) — cache.kartverket.no
  • Lantmäteriet (Sweden) — minkarta.lantmateriet.se

As with any request to a web server, those agencies receive your device's IP address and the coordinates of the map tile requested. We do not control those servers and receive nothing from them. Downloading an area for offline use avoids these requests entirely while you are in that area.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new date.
